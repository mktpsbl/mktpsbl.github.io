---
title: "Why I Chose Keychron B1 Pro As My Travel Keyboard"
date: 2026-11-01
---


Apple Magic Keyboard: None of the function row keys work on Android. The typing feel is different to typing on Macbooks. It feels like tapping on a table, which decreases my typing speed and accuracy. 

Pros: sleekest and most travel friendly

Logitech MX Keys Mini: The function row doesn’t have all the function keys I want, and there is no solution to remap those keys at the hardware level. The actuation force is a little too high; I can see my hand getting tired after a long typing session. The pink didn’t look as premium as I expected. The back of the keyboard is a bit too thick and the keyboard a bit too heavy. There is no wired or 2.4g option, only bluetooth. 

Pros: The look and typing feel is the most premium out of the 3, and I seem to tap very fast on the keyboard. 

Keychron B1 Pro (my choice): It has all the function keys I want, matching the Apple Magic Keyboard. The actuation force is pretty much perfect and I seem to be able to type fast on it like the Logitech MX Keys Mini. It is fairly thin while still having a little bit of a slope. It is also the cheapest option out of the 3. 

Cons: Cheap typing feel and look
